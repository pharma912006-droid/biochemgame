🫀 HEARTCARE

SAVE THE HEART

Shrink into a medical explorer and protect the heart. Cardiovascular health can be protected through informed lifestyle choices, prevention and appropriate medical care.

HOW TO PLAY

Steer with WASD / arrow keys, or drag your finger anywhere on a touch screen. Space (or a double-tap) uses a Pulse: magnet + slow motion.

🍊 Fruit heals · 🥇 Omega-3 bonus score · ⚡ Exercise speed boost · 💧 Water restores energy · 🛡 Shield protects · 💗 Heart orb stores a Pulse · 💊 Medicine heals over time

Avoid cholesterol plaques, blood clots, red-cell traffic and damaged walls. Reach the heart in all 3 levels.

ABOUT HEARTCARE

HEARTCARE is a cardiovascular health awareness project. This game teaches general prevention facts and does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist. No food or supplement cures cardiovascular disease.
